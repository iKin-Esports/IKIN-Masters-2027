# IKIN Masters 2027

## Event Dates
- **Online Play‑In**: January 3 ‑ January 4, 2027
- **LAN Finals**: January 16 ‑ January 17, 2027

- **TO**: iKin Esports
- **Visa Region**: Mainland China
- **VRS Tier**: Tier 2, Open‑registration tournament (open to teams from all global regions)
- **Registration Fee**: **$300 USD**
- **Registration Window**: December 26 ‑ December 30, 2026

> The registration link will be published on December 26th via iKin Github and iKin Web.

- **Team Cap**: Maximum of **16 registered teams**

> 16 teams compete in the Play‑In with the top 4 advancing to the LAN Finals. Seeding is determined by December VRS Rankings.

## Supplementary Provisions

If fewer than 8 teams remain registered after the registration window, the event will be cancelled for failing to meet minimum competition requirements. Full refunds of registration fees will be issued to all registered teams.

## Format
### Play‑In (Online, January 2026)
- 16‑team single‑elimination bracket; all matches are Best‑of‑3 (BO3)
- **Server Nodes**: Hangzhou, Hong Kong

| Date | Item | 
|---|---|
| Jan 3 | Round‑of‑16 | 
| Jan 4 | Round‑of‑8 | 

> Round-of-8 matchs winner advance to LAN Finals.

> Teams would need to comforn attend LAN Finals or not within 24 hours after teams Round-of-8 match ends.

> If team fails to confirm within 24 hours, we will assume they will participate in the LAN Finals. If team exit activity after this time, they will be recorded as a forfeit (FF) in upcoming matchs.

> If team refuse to participate LAN Finals, we will invite 5th-8th Play-In teams from the order of VRS point high to low to substitute for the LAN Finals.

### LAN Finals (Offline, January 2026)
- 4‑team single‑elimination bracket; all matches are Best‑of‑3 (BO3)

| Date | Item | 
|---|---|
| Jan 14 | Arrival Day | 
| Jan 15 | Media Day & Setup | 
| Jan 16 | Semi‑finals | 
| Jan 17 | Grand Final | 

## Prize Pool
| Place | Payout |
|---|---|
| 1st Place | $2,000 USD |
| 2nd Place | $800 USD |
| 3rd‑4th Place | $100 USD |

> iKin Esports will not cover travel costs for participating teams. All travel‑related expenses shall be borne solely by each team.

> During the LAN event, iKin Esports will not provide practice rooms, each team is guaranteed a minimum of 60 minutes for equipment setup and pre‑match warm‑up before going on stage.

## Payment & Registration Rules
- **Payment Methods**: Alipay, WeChat Pay, bank‑issued debit card

> Slot Confirmation: A team slot is only confirmed upon verified receipt of payment by iKin Esports. Payment screenshots do not constitute official confirmation.

### Waitlist Mechanism
> If a team fails to complete payment within 24 hours of submitting its registration, its entry will be given to the next team in the waiting list, ranked by registration time.

> Application Validity: iKin Esports reserves the right to disqualify any team submitting incomplete or erroneous registration forms.

> Official Notices: All tournament‑related communications will be sent exclusively to the email address provided on the registration form.

## Refund & Withdrawal Policy
- **December 26 ‑ December 30, 2026**: 100% registration-fee refund.
- **December 31, 2026 ‑ January 1, 2027**:  80% of the registration fee will be refunded upon withdrawal.
- **January 2, 2027 (Roster Lock)**:  No refunds granted.

> The Online Play-In roster will be locked on January 2, 2026. No withdrawals are permitted after the Roster Lock deadline. Any registered team failing to play its scheduled matches will be recorded as a forfeit (FF), and no registration-fee refund will be issued.

## Registration Eligibility Rule
### Single Organization Rule
The same organization is not permitted to submit two or more teams for this tournament.

### VRS Core Roster Rule
Registered teams must field the core roster featured in the official December 2026 Global VRS rankings for seeding and VRS points.

### iKin Banned
Player can not submit in team to play if he was been banned by iKin Esports.
